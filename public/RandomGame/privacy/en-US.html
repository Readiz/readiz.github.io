1. Game information stays on your device

You can play without signing up or logging in. Touch positions, player numbers, characters, and battle results are handled in your device's memory while the game is running. They are not sent to the developer's servers. Game progress is not retained after the app process ends.

2. Data collection and sharing in the Google Play app

The Google Play version works offline without internet permission. It contains no advertising, analytics, or payment SDKs, and the app does not collect, transmit, or share personal information or device identifiers. Google Play handles installation and updates; its privacy policy applies to those services.

3. Device permissions

The game uses vibration to signal joining and battle results. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or external storage. Only the directly distributed APK adds internet permission to check for updates and permission to open the installer for an APK you choose.

4. Information storage

The developer does not store your account or game history on a server.
